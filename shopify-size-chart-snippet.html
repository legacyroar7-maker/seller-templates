How we measure

  • Chest: flat and buttoned, side seam to side seam, 2.5 cm (1 in) below the armholes, then x 2.
  • Length: on the back, from the top of the shoulder next to the collar down to the hem.
  • Shoulder: from one shoulder seam to the other, across the back.
  • Sleeve: from the shoulder seam to the end of the cuff.

Fit notes

  • Relaxed fit. For a closer fit, take one size down.
  • 100% linen, no stretch.
  • Between two sizes? Take the larger one for a relaxed look, the smaller one for a neater fit.
  • Easy check: lay a shirt you love flat, measure it the same way, and pick the size with the closest numbers.
  • Model is 183 cm (6 ft) tall and wears size M.
  • Measured by hand, so sizes can differ by about 1 cm.
  • Wash at 30°C and dry flat to keep the size.
