The Fernmoor Waxed Canvas Tote is a work bag for people who carry a laptop, lunch and a water bottle every day. It stays upright, closes with a zip and shrugs off light rain.

  • Your laptop stays safe. A padded inside pocket fits most laptops up to 15 inches.
  • Light rain runs off. The cotton canvas is waxed, so drops bead up instead of soaking in.
  • Comfortable on your shoulder. The leather handles have a 28 cm drop, long enough to wear over a coat.

Details

  • Size: 40 x 36 x 12 cm (15.7 x 14.2 x 4.7 in), about 17 litres
  • Weight: 850 g
  • Material: waxed cotton canvas, leather handles, cotton lining
  • Colour: olive green
  • Pockets: padded laptop pocket, zip pocket inside, open pocket outside
  • In the box: 1 tote and a care card

Care

Wipe with a damp cloth and let it dry in the air. Do not put it in the washing machine or tumble dryer. If the canvas starts to look dry, rub in fabric wax once a year.

Questions

Does a 15-inch laptop fit in the Fernmoor Waxed Canvas Tote?
Yes. The padded pocket fits most 15-inch laptops. Check that your laptop is under 36 cm wide.

Is the Fernmoor Waxed Canvas Tote waterproof?
No, it is water-resistant. It handles light rain, but it is not made for heavy rain.

Will the Fernmoor Waxed Canvas Tote look worn over time?
Yes, waxed canvas shows soft creases with use. This is normal for the fabric and not a fault.
